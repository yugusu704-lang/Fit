# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Individuals dedicated to fat loss and body recomposition who prioritize data privacy (strictly offline, zero remote tracking), mathematical precision, and friction-free daily logging.
- **Context of Use**: Quick logging throughout the day (meals, snacks) and workout planning/tracking at home or in the gym/track.
- **Job to Be Done**: Effortlessly input food portions, obtain an automated and balanced exercise plan based on real calorie math (TDEE - deficit), dynamically tweak exercise quantities while maintaining constant total calorie burn, and execute the workout via a tactile pull-out checklist.
- **Emotional Goal**: Quiet confidence, focus, and clarity—free from noisy ads, social feeds, and bloated gamification.

### Brand Personality
- **3-Word Personality**: **Disciplined · Tactile · Pure** (自律 · 触感 · 纯粹)
- **Voice & Tone**: Calm, precise, editorial, respectful. Uses concise, honest microcopy without AI clichés (no "Elevate", "Unleash", or "Seamless").
- **References**: Impeccable Design System, Teenage Engineering hardware UI, Leica camera interfaces, Linear-style utilitarian craftsmanship.
- **Anti-References**: Generic SaaS purple/blue gradients, neon glow effects, card-in-card nesting, bouncy cartoon physics, generic Roboto/Inter default layouts.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light & Dark mode support, both adhering to tinted neutrals (never pure `#000000` or dead `#808080`).
  - **Light Mode**: Canvas `#FBFBFA` (warm bone), Surface `#FFFFFF`, Text `#18181B` (warm off-black), Border `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08)`.
  - **Dark Mode**: Canvas `#121214` (deep charcoal), Surface `#1A1A1E`, Text `#EDEDF0`, Border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Accent Color**: Single restrained accent—Terracotta Ember (Light: `oklch(62% 0.16 35)` / `#D9532F`, Dark: `oklch(68% 0.14 35)` / `#E06D50`), saturation < 75%.
- **Typography**:
  - Headings & Labels: **Outfit** / **Urbanist** (high x-height, distinct geometric clarity).
  - Data, Reps & Calories: **JetBrains Mono** with OpenType tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate layout jitter during dynamic slider rebalancing.
- **Spacing Scale**: Strict 4pt base system (4dp, 8dp, 12dp, 16dp, 24dp, 32dp, 48dp). Touch targets >= 48dp.

### Design Principles
1. **Zero Layout Jitter (Tabular Data Math)**: Real-time re-balancing of exercise quantities and calories must use monospace tabular numbers to prevent horizontal jumps and optical flickering.
2. **Tactile Friction & Intentional Motion (100/300/500)**: Micro-interactions use `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (expo-out); 100ms for button/checkbox feedback, 300ms for sheet transitions. Absolutely no dated rubber-band bounce.
3. **Content Over Containers**: Replace nested cards with macro-spacing, subtle 1px tinted dividers, and typographic scale. Never nest cards inside cards.
4. **Anchored Bottom Sheet as the Tactical Drawer**: Collapsed state serves as a clean progress capsule (`Inflow vs Outflow`); dragging up smoothly reveals sub-workout task cards with custom tactile radio-check rings.
5. **Absolute Offline Privacy**: 100% local SQLite/Room execution. UI states update optimistically within 16ms (60-120fps budget).
